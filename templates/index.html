About GenomeAI

Future Baby Genome Prediction Using Artificial Intelligence

GenomeAI is an Artificial Intelligence powered genome analysis platform developed to analyze maternal and paternal DNA sequences and estimate the health of future babies.

The platform evaluates inherited genetic disorders, predicts disease probability, analyzes genome compatibility, and generates intelligent medical reports using advanced AI algorithms.

GenomeAI was developed as an educational and research platform to demonstrate how Artificial Intelligence can support genetic analysis and healthcare decision-making.
